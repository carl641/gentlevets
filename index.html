Why Gentle Vet

Low-stress handling isn’t a slogan. It’s how we’re trained.

Every person on our team — vets, techs and front desk — is Silver Certified in Dr. Sophia Yin’s Low Stress Handling® methods.

  • Less fear, fewer sedatives. Gentle restraint and counter-conditioning can reduce the need for sedation — lowering risk and cost.
  • A partnership with you. We explain every step, prepare estimates before treatment, and follow up by phone and text afterwards.
  • Modern, well-equipped hospital. In-house digital X-ray, lab and pharmacy, a surgery suite, and advanced anesthetic monitoring.
Silver certification meant 15 hours of lectures, 12 hours of hands-on practice, 10 tests and a final exam — passed by the whole team. Our mission: Experience Care.
Your first visit

What to expect when you come in

Bringing a pet to the vet can be stressful for both of you. Here’s exactly how a visit works, so there are no surprises.

Check in

Our client care team greets you and confirms your contact details so your pet’s records stay accurate.

Meet your technician

A veterinary technician welcomes you into an exam room and asks about diet, medications, and any changes in behavior or appetite.

Exam & plan

Your veterinarian performs a full physical exam, walks you through next steps, and you get a written estimate before any treatment.

FAQs

Good questions, straight answers

Can’t find what you need? Call us at (920) 435-5000 or send a message.

Flexible payment. We accept cash, check, credit cards, CareCredit (special financing on $200+) and Scratchpay plans — checking Scratchpay options won’t affect your credit score.
Are you open on weekends?

No — Gentle Vet is closed Saturdays and Sundays. For after-hours emergencies, please contact BluePearl Fox Valley at (920) 993-9193 or VEG Emergency Vet in Bellevue at (920) 308-3981.

Do I need to pay anything to book as a new client?

Yes. New clients pay a $100 prepay to hold their reservation. The full amount is applied to your appointment total at your visit.

Can I book surgery as my pet’s first appointment?

No. A current exam at Gentle Vet is required before any surgery can be booked, so plan for two visits — an exam first, then a separate surgery date.

Are you Fear Free Certified?

Technically no, but our whole team has completed the Silver level certification in Low Stress Handling®, which we consider superior.

What species will you not see?

Skunks, wildlife, raccoons, foxes, servals, primates, prairie dogs, wolves/wolfdog hybrids, and venomous animals.

How long do health certificates take?

International health certificates can take 1–3 months to process, so please call us early.

Can you take back my unused medications?

Unfortunately we’re not allowed to take custody of dispensed, unused medications. Please use a local drug take-back or disposal location.

Do you offer curbside service?

Yes — if requested, we can still serve you curbside. Just let us know when you book.

Why is my wait time so long?

Like many veterinary practices, we’re experiencing very high call volume. Thank you for your patience with communication and scheduling — booking online is often the fastest route.
